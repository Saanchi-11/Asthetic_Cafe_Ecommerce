Our Story

A little pink corner for ambience lovers

Pink Poney Cafe started as a weekend baking hobby and grew into a neighbourhood favourite. We roast in small batches, bake every morning, and keep our menu short so everything on it is done right.

  • Locally roasted beans
  • Baked fresh, every morning
  • Cozy, laptop-friendly seating
